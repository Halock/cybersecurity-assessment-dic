## 1. Purpose

This document summarizes the data-protection considerations included in the cybersecurity assessment, with reference to the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023**.

The assessment considered how information-security controls, governance, access management, awareness, and risk management support the protection of personal data.

> **Portfolio Note:** This is a sanitized public representation. No personal data, confidential evidence, or sensitive operational information is included.

# 5. Access Control and Authentication

Strong access management helps reduce the risk of unauthorized access to personal information.

The assessment considered:

* User authentication
* Access privileges
* Identity management
* Access reviews
* Security awareness

### Recommended Actions

* Maintain strong authentication requirements.
* Apply least-privilege principles.
* Periodically review access rights.
* Remove unnecessary or inactive access.
* Monitor authentication-related security events.
