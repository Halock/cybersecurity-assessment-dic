## 1. Purpose

This document summarizes the assessment's alignment with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**, the international standard for information-security management systems (ISMS).

The mapping demonstrates how key areas of the cybersecurity assessment relate to information-security governance, risk management, access control, physical security, awareness, and continuous improvement.

> **Portfolio Note:** This is a sanitized public representation. Sensitive operational information, confidential evidence, and detailed infrastructure information have been excluded.

The assessment applied this principle through:

4. Risk classification
5. Risk treatment recommendations
6. Risk monitoring

# 8. Information-Security Improvement Cycle

The assessment supports a continuous improvement approach:

**Assess → Identify → Treat → Monitor → Review → Improve**

This approach allows security controls and risk-management processes to evolve as the organization's technology, operations, and threat environment change.

---

# 9. Key Improvement Areas

The assessment identified several areas where continued improvement could strengthen information-security management:

### Workforce

Increase specialist cybersecurity capability and professional development.

### Governance

Improve security metrics, reporting, and compliance-management automation.

### Risk Management

Maintain continuous risk identification and treatment.

### Physical Security

Continue periodic assessment and optimization of physical-security controls.

### Technology

Maintain continuous validation of authentication, network, monitoring, and protective controls.
