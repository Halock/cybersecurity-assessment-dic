## 7. Risk Treatment Approach

Risk treatment should generally follow this sequence:

1. Identify and document the risk.
2. Assess likelihood and impact.
3. Determine the risk level.
4. Identify appropriate controls.
5. Assign responsibility for treatment.
6. Establish a remediation timeline.
7. Monitor progress.
8. Reassess residual risk.

Risk levels should be reviewed when significant changes occur in the organization's technology, operations, threat environment, or regulatory requirements.
